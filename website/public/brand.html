Forks need their own identity

No fork or other project may use the Vivid name or logo as its branding without blurbery’s prior, explicit written consent. This applies to free and commercial projects, repositories, websites, promotional artwork and app-store listings. Choose your own name and replace Vivid’s brand artwork before presenting or distributing a separate project.

Open source and branding

The source-code licence does not grant permission to use Vivid’s trademarks. Forking the repository or contributing to the app does not grant brand permission. Source-code permissions and required legal notices remain unchanged.

Talking about Vivid

Accurate references, reviews, tutorials and compatibility statements are welcome. Do not present another project as Vivid or imply an official relationship or endorsement.

Ask for permission

Contact admin@vividapp.co. Permission must come from blurbery in writing and applies only to the approved use.
