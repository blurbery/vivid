Privacy

Updated 7 September 2026

This policy covers Vivid and vividapp.co. Your media server and optional connected services have their own privacy policies.

Visiting this website

There are no advertising trackers or analytics scripts on this website. Fonts and images are hosted with the site. I do not ask you to create an account or connect your media server here.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare hosts and protects the website. It processes technical information such as IP addresses and request details to deliver pages and detect abuse. Security checks may use cookies when needed. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for details.

Getting in touch

If you email admin@vividapp.co, your message is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Gmail inbox. Your email address and message are used to respond to your enquiry. Please do not send passwords or media-server credentials.

Your media server

Vivid connects to the Silo or Emby servers you choose. Your server receives your sign-in details and the requests needed for your library, artwork and playback. Vivid sends playback position, pause state and watched-progress updates to that server. Its operator controls those records and may keep connection logs.

Information on your device

Vivid saves account names, selected viewing profiles and preferences locally. Saved account session tokens and optional account PIN records use Keychain; the saved-account feature does not retain your media-server password. Home metadata, artwork and playback buffers may be cached. Downloads you choose are stored on the device. Subtitles and chapters come from the opened media; Vivid does not request external subtitle files or subtitle translation.

IntroDB

IntroDB is enabled by default and can be turned off in Playback settings. Vivid sends the series IMDb ID, season and episode number to api.introdb.app for intro and credits timestamps. IntroDB receives normal connection information, including your IP address, but not your media-server credentials. No IntroDB API key is required.

Trailers and requests

If you configure TMDb, Vivid stores your personal API credential in Keychain and sends it to TMDb with media identifiers to find trailers. Opening a trailer connects to YouTube. If you configure Seerr, Vivid stores its URL, username and password in Keychain to restore that connection. Seerr receives your login, search queries and media requests. These services also receive normal connection information and handle it under their own policies. Disconnecting Seerr removes its saved connection.

Diagnostics and contact

Vivid does not capture or upload in-app diagnostics reports. Technical logs used for development and troubleshooting stay local. The Contact form prepares an email to admin@vividapp.co and does not attach logs or account data automatically. You review and send the message through your mail app.

Your controls

You can clear Home metadata and unused artwork in Settings → Metadata, manage downloads on the device, and turn off optional connections. Sign Out clears the current saved-account session while keeping its card. Removing local data does not delete media, accounts or watch history held by your server; contact its operator about those records. Vivid is still in development, and I’ll update this policy as its features and data handling change.

Questions

Contact admin@vividapp.co with questions or requests about information you have sent me.
