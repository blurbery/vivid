Note: SeerrSettingsIcon is a simplified copy of public/os_icon.svg at that commit. It keeps the icon's shape (the file's first path, unchanged) filled in white, without the gradients and the shading and highlight layers. Vivid shows it as a monochrome template image for Seerr in Settings on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.
Note: The Seerr name and icon identify the Seerr media request service Vivid connects to. Vivid is an independent app and isn't made, endorsed or supported by the Seerr project.
