## Referring to Vivid

You may use the name to discuss Vivid, link to the project, describe compatibility or show the app in reviews and tutorials. Keep references accurate and do not imply that an unrelated product, service or modified build is an official Vivid release.

## Forks and other projects

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No fork or other project may use the Vivid name or logo as its own branding without blurbery’s prior, explicit written consent.** This applies to free and commercial projects, repositories, websites, app icons, screenshots used for promotion, and app-store listings. A fork must choose its own name and replace Vivid’s logo and brand artwork before it is presented or distributed as a separate project.

The source-code licence grants no permission to use Vivid’s trademarks. Downloading or forking the repository, contributing code, or receiving approval for a contribution does not grant brand permission. Do not use confusingly similar branding or imply an official relationship or endorsement.

Request permission from [blurbery](https://github.com/blurbery) at [admin@vividapp.co](mailto:admin@vividapp.co). Permission must identify the approved use; it does not extend to other projects or uses.

These rules reserve Vivid’s brand identity while preserving the source-code permissions in [LICENSE](LICENSE), required legal notices, and lawful factual references to the original project. They do not prevent contributing to Vivid itself.

The [branding guide](docs/branding/README.md) covers logo files, colour and presentation. Server and dependency names identify their respective projects; they do not define Vivid’s app identity.

> [!NOTE]
> This is a project brand policy, not a trademark registration or availability clearance. Use ™ for the brand notice; do not use ® without a confirmed registration. See [IP Australia’s guidance](https://ipfirstresponse.ipaustralia.gov.au/options/use-your-trade-mark-prominently-build-recognition-and-protection).
